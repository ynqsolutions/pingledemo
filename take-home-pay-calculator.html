What Gets Taken Out of a California Paycheck?

Between gross pay and what actually lands in your bank account sits a stack of withholdings: federal income tax, California state income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and California State Disability Insurance (SDI), plus anything you've elected pre-tax like retirement contributions or health insurance. This calculator estimates each of those so you can see roughly where your money goes.

Key Points

  • This is an estimate using standard tax brackets and the standard deduction, not your actual W-4 withholding.
  • California has its own state income tax on top of federal tax, with its own bracket structure.
  • CA SDI is currently withheld at 1.1% with no wage cap.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce taxable income but not Social Security or Medicare wages.
  • If your actual paycheck doesn't match what you expect, that's worth raising with your employer or having reviewed.

Why This Isn't a Paycheck Reconciliation Tool

Real paychecks depend on your specific W-4 elections, your employer's payroll software, any local taxes, wage garnishments, and dozens of other variables this calculator can't see. Use it to get a general sense of your take-home pay, not to double-check your actual pay stub line by line. If something on your real paycheck looks wrong, our guide to reading your paycheck is a good next step.

TL;DR

This estimator applies standard federal and CA tax brackets, FICA, and SDI to your gross pay to estimate take-home pay. It's a rough estimate, not an exact paycheck calculation, actual withholding depends on your W-4 and payroll system.
