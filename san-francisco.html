Local Focus

Common Workplace Issues in San Francisco, CA — San Francisco County

San Francisco's economy runs on tech, hospitality, and healthcare, and each industry tends to generate its own recurring pattern of employment law violations. These are the issues we see most often from workers across San Francisco County, Oakland, Berkeley, Daly City, and South San Francisco.

01

Severance Negotiations

Tech layoffs across San Francisco often come with a severance agreement drafted to protect the company, not you.

→ 02

Independent Contractor Misclassification

Gig and tech workers labeled “1099” are frequently entitled to employee protections under California law.

→ 03

Unpaid Overtime & Wage Theft

Salaried tech employees and hourly hospitality and restaurant staff both lose overtime pay to misclassification.

→ 04

Wrongful Termination After Layoffs

A mass layoff can be used to disguise a termination that was really about your age, leave request, or a complaint you made.

→ 05

Whistleblower Retaliation

San Francisco employees who report fraud, safety issues, or illegal conduct have strong protections under state law.

→ 06

Group Wage & Hour Claims

When a restaurant, hotel, or tech employer underpays one worker, it's usually underpaying many at the same time.
