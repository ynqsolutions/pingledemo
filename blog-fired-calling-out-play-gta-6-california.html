Can You Be Fired for Calling Out to Play GTA 6 in California?

A fake sick day for a big game launch can cost you your job, but not every firing is legal.

TL;DR

Yes. California is an at-will state, so an employer can generally fire you for calling out to play a video game, especially if you lie about being sick or break the attendance policy. Paid sick leave is for health reasons, honest PTO requests are safer, and some firings are still unlawful.

Whenever a huge game like GTA 6 launches, plenty of workers think about calling in sick to play it. The question is fair, and the stakes are real: your paycheck. California protects employees in many situations, but a made-up excuse to skip a shift is rarely one of them. Below, we explain how at-will employment, paid sick leave and PTO apply, when a firing may still be unlawful, and what to do if it happens to you.

1

Short Answer: Yes, It Can Cost You Your Job

California is an state. That means an employer can usually end your job at any time, for any lawful reason or no reason at all. Missing work to play a game is not a protected reason, so an employer who finds out can often discipline or fire you, particularly if you broke the attendance policy or lied about why you were out. Many employers treat dishonesty as a serious offense on its own, even for a first mistake.

Example

A warehouse worker texts her supervisor that she has the flu on launch day, then streams herself playing for six hours. A coworker shows the stream to the manager, and she is fired for lying about being sick. Because she was not using leave for a real health reason, the firing is likely lawful.

2

Calling in Sick When You Aren't Sick

in California is meant for health care, diagnosis, treatment or preventive care for you or a family member, and for certain situations involving domestic violence, sexual assault or stalking. Playing a game does not fit. If you call in sick without a real reason, you are using leave for something it was not meant for, and an employer can usually discipline you for misusing it.

Social media makes this easy to prove. Public posts, streams, coworkers' comments and online gaming status can all show you were not sick. Employers generally cannot demand your social media passwords, but they can act on what is posted publicly.

3

Using PTO or Vacation Instead

Taking an honest day off is a different situation. In California, or PTO is earned pay, and an employer generally cannot make you forfeit time you have already earned. You usually do not owe your employer a reason for using it.

Employers can still set reasonable rules about when time off is taken, such as requiring advance notice or denying a specific date because too many people are out. If your request is approved, you are generally fine. If it is denied and you skip work anyway, the absence can be treated as unexcused, and a no-call, no-show is one of the most common reasons workers are fired.

4

When a Firing Could Still Be Unlawful

At-will does not mean anything goes. A firing may be unlawful if it is for using paid sick leave you were entitled to, or for asking about or complaining about your rights. It may also be unlawful if it is based on a protected trait such as race, sex, age, religion or disability, or if you were treated more harshly than coworkers who did the same thing.

And if you really were sick or caring for a family member, firing you for using protected leave may violate the law. Employers sometimes give a neutral reason, like attendance, for a decision that was really about something else, so the surrounding facts matter.

5

What You're Owed If You Are Fired

Even if the firing is lawful, you still have rights. Your final paycheck is due right away when you are fired, and it must include all accrued, unused vacation or PTO. If the employer pays late, you may be owed extra pay for each day it is late, up to a limit. Learn more about unpaid wages in California.

You can also apply for unemployment benefits. The state decides eligibility, and an employer's claim that you were fired for misconduct can affect it, but you can still apply and explain your side.

6

What to Do

If you want time off for a big launch, ask for PTO early, in writing, and wait for approval before you rely on it. Follow your attendance policy and be honest about why you are out. Do not post about it if you called in sick.

If you are fired, ask for the reason in writing, save your handbook, schedule and messages, and check that your final pay includes your earned PTO.
