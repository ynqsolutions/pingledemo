About Corey A. Pingle

“Being an employee doesn't mean being powerless.”

My name is Corey Pingle and I have helped thousands in employment and personal injury matters over the years. I understand that the legal process can be stressful and confusing. It's my team and I's goal to remove the stress and comfortably walk you through the entire legal process. I strive to get all my clients the best results and the outcome they deserve. I look forward to speaking with you and assisting you with your case.

Before founding the firm, Corey represented employees in cases against businesses of all sizes, from local employers to national corporations. That experience continues to shape the firm today. Every case deserves to be taken seriously, regardless of the size of the employer.

Common Questions

A Few Things People Ask Us

Short answers to what people usually want to know before reaching out.

Do you really work on a no win, no fee basis?

Yes. We take employment cases on contingency, so you pay nothing up front and owe no attorney fees unless we recover money for you.

What happens during the free case review?

We'll ask about what happened, when, and what documentation you have, then tell you honestly whether it looks worth a deeper conversation, no cost, no obligation.

How long does a typical case take?

It varies. Some cases resolve in a few months, others take longer if they go to litigation. We'll give you a realistic estimate once we understand your situation.

Do I need to already know I have a case?

No. Most people aren't sure until they talk to someone. A free case review is a low pressure way to find out where you actually stand.

Can I switch to your firm if I already have a lawyer?

Generally yes, you're free to change representation at any point. We can review your situation and walk you through what that would involve.
